## 1. Deliverables Created (Strict File Ownership)

1. [`server/src/security/securityService.ts`](file:///c:/Users/sanat/OneDrive/Desktop/vela1/server/src/security/securityService.ts)
   - `SecurityService.verifyUserAccess(authenticatedUserId, requestedUserId)`: enforces strict user-level isolation (`D-010`), throwing `SecurityError` with `statusCode = 403` and `code = 'FORBIDDEN_CROSS_USER_ACCESS'` on missing or cross-user access.
   - `SecurityService.encryptText(plain)` & `SecurityService.decryptText(cipher)`: AES-256-GCM authenticated encryption at rest (`enc:v1:<ivHex>:<authTagHex>:<cipherHex>`).
   - `SecurityService.minimizePIForLLM(snapshot)`: strips `nickname`, `concernNotes`, `institution` names, account/obligation/income/goal notes, statement filenames, raw CSV rows (`rawRow`), and scrubs email/IBAN/account number patterns before sending data to LLM prompts (`R015`, `S019`).
   - `SecurityService.computeTransactionFingerprint(txn)` & `SecurityService.deduplicateTransactions(existing, incoming)` + `SecurityService.computeCanonicalHash(value)`.

2. [`server/src/fx/frankfurterService.ts`](file:///c:/Users/sanat/OneDrive/Desktop/vela1/server/src/fx/frankfurterService.ts)
   - `FrankfurterFXService.getLatestRates(base, symbols)` & `getHistoricalRates(dateIso, base, symbols)` using official Frankfurter v1 endpoints (`https://api.frankfurter.dev/v1/latest` and `https://api.frankfurter.dev/v1/${dateIso}`) with fallback to `https://api.frankfurter.app/...`.
   - Evaluates freshness against `policy.horizonAndFreshness.fxRateStaleThresholdHours` (`48` hours) read via `loadPrototypePolicy()`.
   - Never invents a 1:1 rate for unsupported currencies not in the ECB/Frankfurter feed (such as `KGS`), returning `status: 'UNAVAILABLE'`, `rate: 0`.

3. [`server/src/db/sqliteStore.ts`](file:///c:/Users/sanat/OneDrive/Desktop/vela1/server/src/db/sqliteStore.ts)
   - Uses Node 24 built-in `node:sqlite` (`DatabaseSync`, supporting `:memory:` and `server/data/finagent.sqlite`).
   - Creates operational tables (`users`, `pi_states`, `financial_snapshots`, `agent_results`, `evaluation_runs`, `decision_traces`, `recommendations`, `action_feedback`, `fx_rates_cache`, `assistant_messages`) and seeds all 13 canonical AS tables (`as_sources`, `as_financial_claims`, `as_claim_source_mapping`, `as_financial_rules`, `as_financial_formulas`, `as_evidence_gaps`, `as_action_candidates`, `as_action_evidence_mapping`, `as_action_rule_mapping`, `as_action_formula_mapping`, `as_agent_knowledge_mapping`, `as_verification_audit`, `as_version_history`) while preserving `status = 'PENDING_REVIEW'` and `approved_for_production = 'NO'`.
   - Encrypts sensitive user/PI payloads at rest via `SecurityService.encryptText` and enforces `userId` isolation on all queries.

4. [`server/src/orchestrator/reviewWorkflow.ts`](file:///c:/Users/sanat/OneDrive/Desktop/vela1/server/src/orchestrator/reviewWorkflow.ts)
   - `ReviewOrchestrator.computeAgentStaleness` & Step 1 freshness tracking $\operatorname{Stale}(y) = \operatorname{InputChanged}(y) \lor \operatorname{PolicyChanged}(y) \lor \operatorname{Expired}(y)$ for `BehaviorAgent` and `DebtAgent` in `agent_results`.

   - `submitFeedback` lifecycle (`accept`, `adjust` with mandatory re-validation via `ActionChecker.validateCandidate`, `decline` with fresh review rerun excluding the declined action, and `complete` updating PI allocations).
