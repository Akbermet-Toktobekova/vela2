# Recommendation quality assessment after implementation

These are engineering judgments, not financial scores or calibrated performance metrics. They reflect the automated checks and the linked synthetic runs, not production validation.

## 1. PI — 8/10

**What it does:** Supplies confirmed records and their dates to the analyzer and recommendation cycle.

**What improved:** Dated payment coverage is separate from 90-day cash capacity. Confirmed synthetic contributions and balance confirmations can be varied without rewriting the original profiles.

**Next steps:** Add a reviewed product form for contribution intentions and source accounts; evaluate reconciliation and freshness against more real export formats.

**Readiness criterion:** Every material input, intended contribution and freshness state is understandable and editable through the supported product flow.

## 2. Orchestrator — 9/10

**What it does:** Orders analysis, checking, fallback and current-version publication.

**What improved:** Weak valid reviews cannot prevent fallback. Primary clarifications reach Today. Review and monetary feedback share FX resolution. Failed synthetic requests no longer require prematurely closing a running job's database.

**Next steps:** Exercise prolonged provider outages and cancellation under real deployment conditions, beyond synthetic process-local captures.

**Readiness criterion:** Repeated failures, concurrent edits and shutdowns cannot publish stale content or lose the actual blocking reason.

## 3. Financial Analyzer — 8/10

**What it does:** Classifies transactions, interprets observations and supplies deterministic financial and debt metrics.

**What improved:** The generator sees dated funding rather than confusing future income with currently available cash. Existing calculation ownership and policy coefficients remain unchanged.

**Next steps:** Expand fixtures for irregular income, mixed-currency obligations and incomplete contractual debt terms. Independently calibrate existing prototype assumptions.

**Readiness criterion:** All supported cases have reproducible calculations and explicitly bounded uncertainty.

## 4. Primary Generator — 6/10

**What it does:** Proposes specific actions, reviews or clarification questions from PI and applicable AS.

**What improved:** An actual confirmed EUR 500 goal proposal passed semantic checking and the existing financial contract. All nine final baseline slots came from the selected model rather than synthetic filler. Exact field rendering prevents an unsupported date or amount from becoming a trusted fact.

**Remaining weakness:** The original baseline still produced no useful valid primary action: one valid review was weak, seven proposals needed data and one was rejected. The Debt model response contained an uncited salary date and an unsupported account field. A better Today card does not establish that Primary itself is ready.

**Next steps:** Broaden frozen cases and evaluate semantic quality separately from format compliance; improve field selection and distinct clarification behavior without relaxing safety. Supported execution mapping currently covers only two operations.

**Readiness criterion:** Across diverse profiles, useful grounded actions or genuinely decision-blocking clarifications appear reliably without requiring catalog or critical protection to compensate.

## 5. Checker and Critical Coverage — 9/10

**What it does:** Separates structural, semantic and financial eligibility, while discovering critical obligations independently.

**What improved:** Validity and usefulness have separate records. Missing semantic verdicts cannot approve a proposal. EU contact explanations do not invent an applicable external source. The funded Debt pair switches to the payable minimum rather than retaining the shortfall contact.

**Next steps:** Add adversarial language fixtures for disguised execution, wrong event dates and text that claims to resolve a gap without helping resolve it.

**Readiness criterion:** Unsupported claims fail consistently while safe confirmed figures remain usable in reviews.

## 6. Recommendation Engine — 8/10

**What it does:** Applies critical priority, financial scoring and sensitivity checks, then selects a useful safe step or clarification.

**What improved:** Strong's weak electricity review no longer displaces the educational goal. The confirmed contribution receives the existing deterministic impact and score rather than an invented benefit.

**Remaining weakness:** The live complexity batch was incomplete for confirmed Strong, so the explicitly labelled existing baseline was used. A stability result for one scored financial candidate says little about alternatives.

**Next steps:** Improve complexity-output reliability and broaden meaningful eligible alternatives; calibrate existing policy separately, without tuning scores to force approvals.

**Readiness criterion:** Selection is understandable across competing useful candidates, including explicit uncertainty and calibrated prototype assumptions.

## 7. Today and Feedback — 8/10

**What it does:** Displays one selected step and records Accept, Adjust, Decline and separate completion.

**What improved:** EUR 500 Accept and EUR 400 Adjust both passed in the live synthetic workflow. Missing FX blocks a monetary recommendation and is named on Today. A specific clarification can be shown with the existing balance-update route. Source display now preserves empty evidence instead of inventing S001.

**Next steps:** Run interactive web and device checks, especially clarification navigation and adjusted-amount presentation. The saved adjusted record retains the original EUR 500 title alongside `adjustedAmount: 400`; the monetary state is correct, but presentation should make the changed amount consistently clear.

**Readiness criterion:** Users understand the current amount, the reason for the step, what remains blocked, and the difference between intention and completion across both clients.
