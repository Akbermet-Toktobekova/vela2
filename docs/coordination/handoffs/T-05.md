  - `DebtAgent.analyzeDebts` (`server/src/analyzer/debtAgent.ts`) computes all numerical debt metrics (`interest90dBaseline`, `interest12mBaseline`, `payoffMonths`) exclusively via `SharedFinancialCalculator` (`F005–F010`) and assigns urgency labels (`high` for imminent due date within `imminentObligationWindowDays` or `APR >= expensiveDebtThresholdApr`, `medium` for `APR >= 7%`, `low` otherwise).

- **Step 3 (`SuggestionGenerator` — `D-004`):**

  - Excludes actions already completed or declined on the current snapshot.
  - Generates up to 3 diverse executable `CandidateAction`s (`A007`, `A002`, `A012`, `A013`, `A014`, `A015`) with concrete safe amounts bounded by `SharedFinancialCalculator` (`F001–F004, F008, F011–F013`) and canonical `supportingRuleIds`, `supportingClaimIds`, and `supportingSourceIds`.

- **Step 5C (`AIEvaluationService` — `D-008`):**

## 4. Discovered Formula / Rule Limitations or Edge Cases
- **Day-0 Outflow vs. 90-Day Cumulative `F003`:** `F003` (`availableCashBeforeActionF003`) sums confirmed inflows across the full 90-day horizon, whereas immediate cash actions (`A002`, `A007`, `A008`, `A012`) reduce cash on Day 0 in `F004` (`projectNetCashTrajectory90d`). To prevent proposing a Day-0 amount that dips below `bufferFloor` before a mid-month paycheck arrives, `SuggestionGenerator` bounds immediate outflows by `min(availableCashBeforeActionF003, minProjectedCashSignedF004 - bufferFloor)`.
- **Dual Keying in `AIEvaluationService`:** `evaluateCandidateComplexities` indexes its returned record by both `candidate.candidateId` and `candidate.actionId` so downstream `ComplexityEvaluator` consumers can look up scores by either key seamlessly.
